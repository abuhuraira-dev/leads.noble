# Noblesoft — simple daily guide

1. **Open your studio.** Sign in with your admin password (same on your phone). Android: browser menu → Install app. iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
2. **Find a small group.** Discover → choose one industry, city and country code (PK, GB, US, AE etc.). Start with 10–15 businesses. Click Discover & research and keep the tab open. If a city returns nothing, use coordinates or import a list.
3. **Review the best candidates.** Leads → click a business. Check the source and website. Confirm industry, location, public contact details and a specific opportunity. Do not treat a missing website field as proof there is no website. Record what you checked.
4. **Save verification.** A qualified business has the right industry, location, checked contact and an actual need. An existing website may need appointment booking, enquiries, client onboarding or other automation; describe only what you verified.
5. **Write a useful offer.** Open the Offer tab → Generate offer. Edit it, review the recipient and claims, mark reviewed and save. Open email app and send from your own account. Afterwards mark Contacted in the pipeline.
6. **Track the next step.** Details → set follow-up date and notes. Today shows due follow-ups. When they reply, change the stage. Stop follow-ups on replies and permanently suppress opt-outs with Do not contact. The app does not read your inbox or send automatically.
7. **Invoice an agreed project.** Details → Create linked invoice. Edit service, prices, currency, business billing address, tax, payment instructions and due date. Save to studio. In the invoice editor use Print / Save PDF to create your final invoice.
8. **Back up.** Settings & backups → Download full backup. Use Export CSV when you need a spreadsheet. Restore imports missing leads for a fresh review and restores invoices as separate copies.

## If something fails

- **No results:** Try another industry or use coordinates with a 2–5 km radius. Mapping coverage varies. Use a CSV with `name,category,location,website,email,phone,source` for businesses you researched yourself.
- **Source busy:** Wait a minute. Retry the discovery search. There is no CAPTCHA bypass or paid fallback.
- **Research failed:** The lead is saved. Retry from its Research tab or run history. If the website blocks crawling, inspect it yourself and record your evidence.
- **Stopped midway:** Open Discover → Run history → Resume / retry. The tab must stay open for research to continue.
- **Not qualified:** Confirm every fit check, a usable contact and a concrete opportunity, then save verification.
- **Blank sender or invoice placeholders:** Fill sender details in Settings and enter your real billing and payment details before sending anything.
- **Offline:** Reconnect; records are stored in the private database, not in an offline phone copy.

**First-day target:** 10 businesses found, 3 carefully verified, 1 personal offer, 1 planned follow-up. No ads needed.
